# Kadia Systems Group — mark

PRODUCT.md records that the **product** has no decided name or identity. This
is the mark for **KSG, the company that builds it**, and that is how it is
used: it sits in the application chrome as the builder's mark, never as a
product logo.

## The mark

Two staggered brackets, interlocking — filled, symmetrical about the centre,
with the counter left open. The stagger is the idea: level brackets read as a
literal `[]`, and the offset turns them into two linked forms instead. Two
things held together is the whole business — a centre and the season it has to
get through.

Construction follows heidihealth.com, pinned by the user: a filled symmetrical
glyph rather than a stroked letterform, set beside a serif wordmark.

- `ksg-mark.svg` — mark alone, `currentColor`, 37px grid
- `ksg-lockup.svg` — mark plus KSG in Instrument Serif
- `ksg-app-icon.svg` — ink tile with the mark in butter, for favicons

## Using it

- Ink `#28030F` on paper, or butter `#FBF582` on ink. Nothing else.
- The wordmark is **Instrument Serif, roman** — the same family as the display
  accent, but never the italic. The italic belongs to content.
- Clear space on all sides is the width of one bracket arm.
- Smallest size is 16px. Below that the stagger closes and it reads as a blob.
- Do not level the two brackets, and do not close the counter.

## Rejected, so it is not tried again

- **A K whose arms were a check**, meant to read as "cleared". A K needs
  terminals at top-right and bottom-right; a check has neither, so it read as
  neither. Abandoned.
- **A cut K.** Legible, but a stroked monogram sits in a different register
  from the reference and looked thinner beside the serif wordmark.
- **A quatrefoil.** Read as a medical cross — right for the reference's
  industry, wrong for this one.
